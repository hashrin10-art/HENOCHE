The idea

One mark. No noise. Made to be worn until it holds your shape.

Henoche started with a plain black tee and a harder question: what do you actually want people to remember about you? Not a slogan. Not a hype drop. Just fabric that fits right and a single embroidered H, because the story is yours to tell — ours is just to get out of the way.

Two colours. No noise.
