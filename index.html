Our Story

Every stitch carries
a story worth wearing.

HENOCHE was built on a simple idea: clothing should feel as considered as it looks. Each piece is cut from heavyweight cotton, finished with hand-embroidered detailing, and marked with a monogram designed to age well — on a hoodie, a hangtag, or a label you'll notice every time you get dressed.

Minimal. Modern. Timeless. We keep the design language quiet so your story can be loud.
